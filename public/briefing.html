01 What we heard

Event shoppers struggle to find or confirm the right items quickly.

Reviews point to the same issue: discovery, availability, and store confidence are split across the journey.

Customer

Occasion-led demand

"I need something for a winter wedding" is a better brief than "black dress".

Store

Availability uncertainty

Customers and associates still need to confirm store, size, substitutes, and timing.

Business

High-intent friction

Event shoppers have urgency, higher basket potential, and lower tolerance for failed searches.

Lost conversionhigh-intent shoppers abandon when availability confidence is low
Manual lookupassociates bridge style, stock, substitutes, and customer messaging
Brand trustpoor reviews compound when store discovery falls short of the digital promise

02 Engagement gap

The interface matters: chat captures more of the buying mission.

Customers are more likely to engage when they can explain the occasion in their own words.

Core point

OpenAI gives RetailNext a conversational front door for product discovery and a structured bridge into retail systems.

03 Assistant approach

An inventory-grounded AI assistant for event shopping.

OpenAI interprets text, images, and follow-up chat. RetailNext verifies products, stock, price, and fulfilment.

UnderstandText, image, occasion, style, budget, store
RetrieveSemantic catalogue matches
GroundSKU, inventory, price, size, fulfilment
ConverseSwap, explain, refine, email
ActivateBasket, associate handoff, CRM follow-up
OpenAI role

Understand and converse

Intent extraction, image understanding, semantic retrieval inputs, action selection, explanations, and review.

RetailNext role

Control retail decisions

Catalogue SKUs, price, stock, store rules, basket changes, fulfilment, and CRM delivery.

06 Architecture

Flexible conversation, controlled retail decisions.

Click through the flow to show the OpenAI and RetailNext boundary.

OpenAI API powered

  • Text and image understanding
  • Structured JSON shopping briefs
  • Embeddings for semantic retrieval
  • Tool patterns for chat actions
  • Grounded generation for shopper and associate copy

RetailNext owned

  • Customer surfaces and basket state
  • Catalogue, product images, prices, and inventory
  • Budget, availability, substitutes, and fulfilment rules
  • Preview/apply safety for basket changes
  • Business KPIs, channel deployment, and CRM handoff

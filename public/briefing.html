01 What we heard

Event shoppers struggle to find or confirm the right items quickly.

Reviews point to the same issue: discovery, availability, and store confidence are split across the journey.

Customer

Occasion-led demand

"I need something for a winter wedding" is a better brief than "black dress".

Store

Availability uncertainty

Customers and associates still need to confirm store, size, substitutes, and timing.

Business

High-intent friction

Event shoppers have urgency, higher basket potential, and lower tolerance for failed searches.

Lost conversionhigh-intent shoppers abandon when availability confidence is low
Manual lookupassociates bridge style, stock, substitutes, and customer messaging
Brand trustpoor reviews compound when store discovery falls short of the digital promise

02 Engagement gap

The interface matters: chat captures more of the buying mission.

Customers are more likely to engage when they can explain the occasion in their own words.

Core point

OpenAI gives RetailNext a conversational front door; RetailNext systems keep the answer tied to real stock and approved actions.

06 Architecture

Flexible agent, controlled actions.

The agent can call only approved tools: retrieval, inventory, basket, engagement and review.

OpenAI API powered

  • Text and image understanding
  • Structured JSON shopping briefs
  • Embeddings for semantic retrieval
  • Tool selection and structured action requests
  • Grounded copy and evals for quality, latency and cost

RetailNext owned

  • Approved APIs or MCP servers for inventory, basket and engagement
  • Catalogue, product images, prices, stores and inventory
  • Budget, availability, substitutes, and fulfilment rules
  • Preview/apply safety for basket changes
  • Pilot design, ROI targets, channel deployment and email handoff

07 Why OpenAI

08 Business outcomes

Business value map for RetailNext.

The target is measurable ROI, not a novelty chat surface.

Experience Better engagement

Natural language lowers the effort to start.

Revenue Stronger event conversion

Inventory-grounded outfits increase basket confidence.

Operations Less manual lookup

Associates get item rationale and backup options.

Reuse More value from one build

The same tool and eval pattern can support web, store, messaging and recovery journeys.

Pilot scorecard

Relevant precedent: OpenAI worked with Zalando on an assistant for product discovery, with evals as a key part of improving quality. RetailNext should use the same discipline: A/B test 10% of event-shopping traffic, then scale when lift covers cost.

09 Rollout

Crawl, walk, run.

Prove the ROI first, then expand the same assistant pattern across the wider commerce journey.

0-8 weeks

Crawl

2-4 months

Walk

Scale

Run

Scale across ecommerce, associate tools, WhatsApp and email once ROI is clear.
